# Acoustic Folk

## Identity

Story-first music built on acoustic instruments, natural room sound, singable melody, and lyrical detail.

## Default model

`v6` for narrative consistency; `v6-mini` can test melody and lyric density.

## Useful descriptors

Fingerpicked guitar, wooden room, warm harmonies, storytelling, gentle mandolin, soft upright bass, campfire intimacy, earthy percussion.

## Instrumentation

Acoustic guitar or fingerpicked ukulele/banjo, upright bass, light percussion, fiddle or mandolin accents, harmony vocals, and occasional piano.

## Vocal direction

Natural, grounded, and narrative. The singer should sound close to the listener, with clear consonants and restrained ornamentation.

## Rhythm and production

Around 76–108 BPM. Keep dynamics human, leave breath and room sound, and avoid over-quantizing the strum or vocal phrasing.

## Avoid

Cinematic strings in every verse, fake rustic clichés, long verses with no chorus, overly polished pitch correction, and a rhythm section that buries lyrics.

## Chinese/English notes

Chinese folk lyrics benefit from concrete objects and simple syntax. Avoid archaic phrasing unless the entire concept intentionally supports it.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original acoustic folk song telling a small true-to-life story. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft upright bass, gentle percussion, one mandolin or fiddle accent, warm natural harmonies. Grounded close lead vocal with clear storytelling. Avoid glossy pitch correction and oversized cinematic strings.
```
