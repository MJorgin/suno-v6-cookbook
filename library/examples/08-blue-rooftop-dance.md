## Goal
Create a clean euphoric dance track with a short bilingual rooftop-at-dawn hook.

## Model
`v6` for controlled build and drop arrangement.

## Inputs
Short original vocal lines, section cues, and one-drop limitation.

## Style Prompt
Original electronic dance, punchy four-on-the-floor kick, tight bass, sidechain chords, bright lead synth, eight-bar build, one euphoric drop idea, wide club mix, short repeatable bilingual hook.

## Exclude Styles
Multiple competing drops, harsh noise, unlicensed vocal fragments, and dense verse storytelling.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Build]
天亮以前
Hold the line
风在呼吸
Feel the light
[Drop Vocal]
Blue rooftop, 心跳
Blue rooftop, 燃烧
[Breakdown]
Only kick and bass for one bar, then all synths return.
```

## Expected Result
A physical club groove with a simple Chinese/English hook and one clear release.

## Failure Modes
Chinese vowels get buried, the drop contains unrelated ideas, or vocals compete with the lead synth.

## Next Iteration
Use Chinese only on the sustained hook and English only as rhythmic pre-drop stabs.

Evidence tier: `working-hypothesis`.
