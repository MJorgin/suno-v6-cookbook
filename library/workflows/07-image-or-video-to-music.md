# Image or Video to Music

## When to use

Use this when a visual asset provides the mood, pacing, color palette, scene, or narrative cue for a new track.

## Recommended model

- First interpretation: `v6-wild`
- Controlled final version: `v6`

## Inputs

- An image or video you own or have rights to use
- Visual elements that matter
- Desired duration and use case
- Language or instrumental preference
- Whether motion, color, or character emotion drives the music

## Recipe

1. Confirm rights to the image or video and avoid private footage unless everyone depicted permits the use.
2. Name three visual elements: light, motion, and emotional subject.
3. State whether the output should follow cuts, ambient movement, or inner emotion.
4. Use `v6-wild` for two broad interpretations.
5. Select one and use `v6` for melody, structure, and production control.

## Copy-ready template

```text
Create original music inspired by my attached <image/video>. I own or have rights to use this source.

Visual anchors:
- Light/color: <description>
- Motion: <slow/fast/steady/abrupt>
- Emotional subject: <feeling or scene>

Music target:
<instrumental or language>, <genre>, <tempo>, <instruments>, <duration>.

Follow <visual rhythm / emotional mood / narrative arc>.
Do not add literal sound effects unless they are subtle and musical.
```

## What to preserve

Preserve the visual mood and pacing logic rather than asking for a literal sonic copy of every object on screen.

## Failure signals

- The track is packed with literal sound effects.
- The mood contradicts the visual lighting or movement.
- Video edits cause disjointed song sections.
- A vocal language appears despite requesting instrumental music.

## Next iteration

Remove object-by-object descriptions, keep one visual metaphor, and specify a clear song or cue structure.

## Evidence

Evidence tier: `official` for image/video multimodal input; `official-informed` for the two-stage interpretation workflow.
