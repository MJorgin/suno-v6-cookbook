# Bossa and Jazz Pop

## Identity

Sophisticated but relaxed music with harmonic color, soft swing or bossa groove, brushed drums, and conversational melody.

## Default model

`v6` for harmony and vocal balance; `v6-wild` only for subtle harmonic alternatives.

## Useful descriptors

Nylon-string guitar, brushed drums, warm upright bass, seventh chords, café intimacy, soft ride cymbal, smoky piano, relaxed phrasing.

## Instrumentation

Nylon-string guitar, jazz piano, upright bass, brushed snare, ride cymbal, muted trumpet or saxophone accents, and light background vocals.

## Vocal direction

Soft, behind-the-beat, intimate, and precise. The vocal should glide rather than shout; consonants should remain light.

## Rhythm and production

Around 80–118 BPM. Keep dynamic range open, panned guitar and piano distinct, bass round, and drums low in volume.

## Avoid

Horns playing through the entire song, loud electronic drums, aggressive swing, sampled jazz recordings, and overly complex lyrics with no space.

## Chinese/English notes

Chinese bossa vocals need extra space because soft jazz phrasing can blur dense characters. English or Portuguese-style syllables are optional; do not request a named vocalist.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original relaxed bossa/jazz-pop song. Nylon-string guitar, warm jazz piano, round upright bass, brushed drums and soft ride, occasional muted horn accent. Intimate lead vocal slightly behind the beat, rich seventh-chord harmony, small-club closeness. Avoid loud electronic drums and nonstop horn solos.
```

## Evidence

Evidence tier: `working-hypothesis`; harmony and phrasing quality should be compared across generations.
