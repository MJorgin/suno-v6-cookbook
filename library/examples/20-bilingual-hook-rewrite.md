## Goal
Rewrite a crowded bilingual chorus so Chinese carries emotion and English carries rhythmic lift.

## Model
`v6` for polished pronunciation and phrasing after a quick `v6-mini` diction test.

## Inputs
Original weak hook, language roles, line-length target, and synth-pop direction.

## Style Prompt
Original bilingual Cantonese-tinged electropop, glassy synths, tight electronic drums, melodic bass, clean center vocal, open Chinese emotional hook, short English rhythm answers.

## Exclude Styles
Long English sentences in the chorus, random language switches, harsh autotune, and rap verses that obscure the hook.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
Rewrite for singability.
Language rule: Cantonese carries the emotional message; English appears only as a two-syllable rhythmic answer.
Weak idea: "Don't say goodbye because I still believe tomorrow will shine for us tonight"
New hook:
[Chorus]
別講再會
Hold light
霓虹海裡
All right
心照不宣
One night
明天總會
More bright
```

## Expected Result
Short alternating phrases with stable vowel endings and one clear repeated title concept.

## Failure Modes
English becomes a full sentence, Chinese sounds crowded, or the language switch lacks a musical reason.

## Next Iteration
Remove half the English, place it in background vocals only, and keep one Chinese phrase at the highest note.
