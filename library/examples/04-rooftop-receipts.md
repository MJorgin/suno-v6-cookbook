## Goal
Create a confident bilingual trap track about rebuilding after setbacks without threats or hostile boasting.

## Model
`v6` for beat/vocal balance and clearer control of the bilingual hook.

## Inputs
Original bilingual lyrics, sparse trap direction, and content safety boundaries.

## Style Prompt
Original bilingual trap, deep rounded 808, rolling hi-hats, sparse minor-key electric piano, crisp clap, ambient city texture, close controlled rap, melodic half-sung hook, steady pocket.

## Exclude Styles
Named artist imitation, violent threats, uncleared samples, crowded samples, and aggressive scream rap.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Verse]
从天台往下看
灯把弯路照明白
旧账单折成纸飞机
飞过大雨和牌樓
I keep the quiet, I keep the count
小步也走到街口
[Hook]
Rise up, 慢慢来
Night falls, 我还在
Rise up, count the days
光会落在我口袋
```

## Expected Result
A clean pocket, simple bilingual hook, and sparse beat that leaves room for the words.

## Failure Modes
Awkward English stress, too many hi-hat rolls, or lyrics that imply real violence.

## Next Iteration
Move English to two-syllable hits, reduce the verse rhyme density, and test with a simpler drum pattern.

Evidence tier: `working-hypothesis`; bilingual cadence requires listening tests.
