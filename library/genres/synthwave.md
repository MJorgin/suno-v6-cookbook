# Synthwave

## Identity

Retro-futuristic electronic music with neon atmosphere, motorik drums, arpeggiated synthesizers, and cinematic melancholy.

## Default model

`v6-wild` for texture first, then `v6` for structure and vocal control.

## Useful descriptors

Neon highway, analog synths, gated reverb drums, arpeggio pulse, nocturnal nostalgia, widescreen chorus, magnetic tape sheen.

## Instrumentation

Analog bass synth, drum machine, gated snare, rising arpeggios, warm pads, electric guitar with delay, and sparse lead synth.

## Vocal direction

Breathy, controlled, and slightly distant in verses; open and layered in the chorus. Treat the voice as another synthesizer line without losing intelligibility.

## Rhythm and production

Around 100–120 BPM with a steady four-on-the-floor or driving downbeat. Use long reverb spaces, sidechain movement, and a strong low-end pulse.

## Avoid

Cartoonish retro sound effects, nonstop guitar solos, muddy reverb, unreadable lyrics, and stacking every 1980s production cliché at once.

## Chinese/English notes

English often carries the genre's detached phrasing naturally. For Chinese, shorten lines and avoid dense literary imagery behind heavy reverb.
