# Lo-fi Bedroom

## Identity

Soft, imperfect, intimate music built on mellow chords, dusty drums, quiet vocals, and small-room atmosphere.

## Default model

`v6-mini` for quick sketches, then `v6` for a balanced finished version.

## Useful descriptors

Dusty keys, mellow guitar, tape hiss, soft saturation, lazy drums, rainy window, whispered closeness, simple chord loop.

## Instrumentation

Electric piano, jazz-influenced guitar chords, soft bass, brushed or boom-bap drums, room noise, ambient rain only if subtle, and minimal piano.

## Vocal direction

Close, quiet, breathy, and unpolished in a deliberate way. Doubling should be soft; avoid aggressive pitch effects unless requested.

## Rhythm and production

Around 70–92 BPM. Slight swing, sparse percussion, controlled low end, and dynamic breathing room. Imperfection should sound warm, not careless.

## Avoid

Over-mastered drops, constant sound effects, distracting rain, complicated lyrics, aggressive ad-libs, and a full horn section.

## Chinese/English notes

Chinese lyrics work especially well with everyday language and short images. English phrases can be half-spoken but should not hide the main Chinese hook.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original lo-fi bedroom song about <quiet everyday feeling>. Mellow electric piano or guitar, soft bass, lazy brushed drums, subtle tape warmth, small-room closeness. Quiet breathy lead vocal, simple lyrics, no big drop. Avoid distracting rain effects, loud ad-libs, and glossy EDM production.
```

## Evidence

Evidence tier: `official-informed`; the model route favors quick drafts for simple loops and flagship refinement for finished vocals.
