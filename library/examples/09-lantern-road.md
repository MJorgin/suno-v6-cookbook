## Goal
Create a Chinese acoustic folk song about walking home with a paper lantern during a family reunion.

## Model
`v6` for narrative consistency and natural acoustic arrangement.

## Inputs
Original concrete lyrics and fingerpicked instrumentation.

## Style Prompt
Original Mandarin acoustic folk, fingerpicked guitar, soft upright bass, gentle hand percussion, faint mandolin accent, warm natural room sound, grounded close lead vocal, quiet family harmonies after the second chorus.

## Exclude Styles
Oversized strings, glossy pitch correction, electronic drums, archaic poetry, and rushed phrasing.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Verse]
灯笼晃过石板路
锅里热着老豆腐
爷爷不说想念谁
只把碗筷多摆一副
[Chorus]
回家的路不算长
弯过几条旧时光
灯还亮在老地方
照着童年的模样
```

## Expected Result
A specific, believable story with simple language and a warm acoustic chorus.

## Failure Modes
Lyrics become too literary, percussion overpowers the story, or harmonies enter too early.

## Next Iteration
Remove one verse detail and ask for the harmonies to enter only during the final chorus.

Evidence tier: `working-hypothesis`.
