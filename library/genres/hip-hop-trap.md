# Hip-Hop and Trap

## Identity

Rhythm-first music built on strong 808 motion, sparse melodic loops, confident cadence, and space for vocal personality.

## Default model

`v6` for beat/vocal balance; `v6-wild` for experimental ad-libs and darker textures.

## Useful descriptors

Deep 808, rolling hi-hats, minor-key melody, sparse space, steady pocket, dark atmosphere, crisp clap, melodic half-sung hook.

## Instrumentation

808 bass, sparse piano or guitar loop, hi-hat rolls, snare/clap, ambient pad, and one signature sound. Keep the arrangement sparse enough for the vocal.

## Vocal direction

Controlled, rhythmic, and close. Use a compact verse flow and a repeated melodic hook. Ad-libs should answer the main line, not overwhelm it.

## Rhythm and production

Common range 130–160 BPM with half-time feel. Build movement through 808 rhythm and hi-hat subdivision rather than constant new instruments.

## Avoid

Unsafe threats, real-person impersonation, uncleared samples, five rappers in one prompt, busy melodies, and lyrics with no room to breathe.

## Chinese/English notes

Mandarin or Cantonese rap needs stronger rhythmic anchors than tonal rhyme alone. Keep end sounds simple and use English only as a short cadence hit unless the performer is bilingual.
