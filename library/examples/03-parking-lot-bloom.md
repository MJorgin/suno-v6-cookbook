## Goal
Create a bright English indie-pop song about finding hope after an ordinary bad day.

## Model
`v6` for a clean arrangement and repeatable chorus.

## Inputs
Original English lyrics and guitar/synth production direction.

## Style Prompt
Original English indie pop, jangly electric guitar, melodic bass, simple live drums, soft synth pad, light shaker, dry intimate verse, bright sing-along chorus, wistful optimism.

## Exclude Styles
Broadway belting, EDM festival drums, heavy distortion, and abstract poetry.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Verse]
I found a flower in the parking lot
Growing through the faded white line
Maybe something small can beat the dark
Maybe I can make it this time
[Chorus]
Open the window, let the morning through
Even the concrete holds a drop of blue
Open the window, I'm coming home to you
```

## Expected Result
A compact hook, conversational verses, and a natural lift without a glossy electronic drop.

## Failure Modes
Over-sentimental lyrics, a weak chorus repeat, or a mix that makes the vocal distant.

## Next Iteration
Make the hook six syllables or fewer, remove one instrument, and regenerate with one explicit chorus repeat.

## Evidence
Evidence tier: `official-informed`.
