# Electronic Dance

## Identity

High-energy, club-ready electronic music built for build, release, repetition, and physical movement.

## Default model

`v6` for arrangement control; `v6-wild` for riser, texture, and drop variations.

## Useful descriptors

Punchy four-on-the-floor, sidechain pad, bright lead, clean build, euphoric hook, club mix, wide stereo sweep, concise vocal sample-like phrase.

## Instrumentation

Kick, clap, hi-hats, bass synth, chord stab, riser, lead hook, and sparse vocal chops only from original or cleared material.

## Vocal direction

Short and rhythmic. Use a memorable phrase before the drop and simple repeated hooks. Long emotional stories belong in a separate intro or radio version.

## Rhythm and production

Common range 122–130 BPM for house and 128–140 for broader festival styles. Use clear build, one drop idea, a groove reset, and one final lift.

## Avoid

Multiple drop ideas at once, harsh noise, unlicensed vocal fragments, lyrics too dense for dancing, and three genre switches in one track.

## Chinese/English notes

Short Chinese hooks can work if vowel sounds are open. English is often easiest for rhythmic stabs, but bilingual songs need one dominant language per section.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original electronic dance track with a euphoric but clean energy. Punchy four-on-the-floor kick, tight bass, sidechain chords, bright lead, simple build, one strong drop idea, wide club mix. Short repeatable vocal hook in <language>. Use only original vocal fragments; avoid multiple drops and harsh noise.
```
