## Goal
Explore a bittersweet Cantonese electropop song inspired by a late ferry and neon harbor light.

## Model
Start with `v6-wild` for synth textures and unexpected melodic turns.

## Inputs
A visual scene, genre direction, and short Cantonese hook.

## Style Prompt
Original Cantonese electropop, neon harbor night, glassy arpeggios, tight electronic drums, melodic synth bass, rhythm guitar stabs, airy harmonies, clean modern city sheen.

Disco clichés, harsh vocal effects, retro-only production, and dense English rap.
