# English Indie Pop

## Identity

Bright, personal guitar-and-synth pop with conversational lyrics, tidy hooks, and a balance between bedroom intimacy and festival lift.

## Default model

`v6` for a polished first pass; `v6-wild` for unusual instrumentation or structural surprises.

## Useful descriptors

Jangly electric guitar, handclaps, wistful optimism, airy synth, melodic bass, bedroom intimacy, sing-along chorus, dry close vocal.

## Instrumentation

Clean or slightly chorused electric guitar, bass, straightforward drum kit, soft synth pad, simple countermelody, and light percussion.

## Vocal direction

Conversational and slightly vulnerable. Prioritize intelligible words, casual consonants, and one big repeated melodic phrase in the chorus.

## Rhythm and production

Around 100–124 BPM. Use a steady groove, dynamic verse/chorus contrast, and a mix that feels human rather than over-quantized.

## Avoid

Broadway-style belting, wall-of-sound clutter, abstract lyrics with no repeated hook, and overly glossy EDM drums.

## Chinese/English notes

For bilingual versions, keep English verses syllable-light. Put Chinese in the emotional chorus only if the line has a stable repeated vowel sound.
