# Safety and Copyright

This is an independent community cookbook for Suno v6 workflows. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Suno.

## What This Repository Does

- Provides original text prompts, lyric structures, model-routing guidance, and
  workflow recipes.
- Asks users to copy prompts into Suno themselves.
- Encourages sources and audio that the user owns, created, or has permission
  to use.
- Converts references to named performers into non-identifying technical
  descriptors.

## What This Repository Does Not Do

- It does not log into Suno or automate account actions.
- It does not collect passwords, cookies, session tokens, or API keys.
- It does not call private, unofficial, or resold Suno APIs.
- It does not spend credits, publish songs, upload media, or download output on
  a user's behalf.
- It does not teach exact voice cloning, artist impersonation, or evasion of
  platform safety controls.

## Named Artists and Voices

An artist reference can be useful as a private starting point, but public
recipes should translate it into elements such as:

- era and scene;
- tempo, groove, and rhythm;
- instrumentation and arrangement;
- recording space and production texture;
- vocal range, timbre, delivery, and pronunciation style;
- emotional stance and narrative perspective.

Avoid prompts that ask for a specific real person's exact voice, a performer
impersonation intended to deceive listeners, or a named artist's unreleased
material.

## Source Material

Mashup, sampling, isolation, image, video, and voice-memo workflows assume the
user owns the source or has the necessary rights. Do not upload:

- commercial recordings or stems ripped from released songs;
- private recordings without the participant's permission;
- copyrighted lyrics presented as original work;
- images or videos containing unauthorized recognizable people, brands, or
  media;
- source files whose provenance cannot be explained.

## Prompt Claims

Prompt quality is probabilistic. Use wording such as "recommended for", "more
likely to", "test before relying on", and "evidence level". Do not promise
commercial release readiness, platform approval, income, or an exact sonic
match.

## Takedown Process

If a contribution appears to infringe rights or violate these rules, open an
issue identifying the file and concern. Maintainers may remove the content
first and review attribution or provenance afterward.
