## Goal
Create a 12-second original intro sting for a technology-and-culture podcast.

## Model
`v6` for concise structure and polished sonic identity.

## Inputs
Duration, mood, instrumental requirement, and non-lyrical vocal identity.

## Style Prompt
Original 12-second podcast intro, curious and modern, soft analog pulse, clean electric piano chord, subtle granular shimmer, warm sub-bass, one memorable three-note motif, gentle whoosh made musical, confident but not corporate.

## Exclude Styles
Full lyrics, cinematic trailer impacts, generic corporate ukulele, loud alarm sounds, and long build-ups.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
Create a 12-second instrumental intro sting.
Structure:
0:00–0:03 soft pulse and one high texture
0:03–0:08 three-note motif with electric piano and sub-bass
0:08–0:12 clean resolution with a small lift, ready for speech

No sung lyrics. Use only a short wordless "ah" if vocals are needed.
```

## Expected Result
A compact, speech-friendly motif with a clear ending and space for voice-over.

## Failure Modes
The cue lasts too long, has no resolution, or includes distracting lyrics and effects.

## Next Iteration
Request stems-style separation mentally: motif only, pulse only, and ending only; choose the strongest eight seconds.
