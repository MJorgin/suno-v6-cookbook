# Mandopop Ballad

## Identity

Emotionally clear, melody-forward Chinese pop built around intimate verses and a wide, memorable chorus.

## Default model

`v6` for pronunciation and emotional continuity; `v6-mini` only for fast line-length tests.

## Useful descriptors

Warm piano, urban night, restrained heartbreak, clean electric guitar, gradual strings, intimate breath tone, soaring chorus, modern Mandopop ballad.

## Instrumentation

Piano or clean electric piano in the verse, soft synthesizer pads, brushed drums or slow modern beat, bass that enters late, layered strings in the final chorus.

## Vocal direction

Start close and conversational. Use light vibrato at phrase ends, a controlled chest voice in the chorus, and stacked but transparent harmonies. Keep diction natural rather than theatrical.

## Rhythm and production

Slow to mid tempo, around 68–82 BPM. Leave space in the first verse, add drums at the pre-chorus, and expand width only at the final chorus.

## Avoid

Overcrowded lyrics, translated poetry, constant cymbal washes, opera-style delivery, accidental English phrasing, and a drum loop stronger than the melody.

## Chinese/English notes

Prefer common Mandarin word order. Keep verse lines around 8–12 Chinese characters when possible and place the emotional keyword at a line end. English works best as a short background hook.
