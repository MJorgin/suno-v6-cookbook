## Goal
Create an uplifting rock anthem about speaking honestly after a long period of distance.

## Model
`v6` for riff, live-drum balance, and a controlled chorus.

## Inputs
Original English lyrics and guitar-driven direction.

## Style Prompt
Original uplifting rock anthem, crunchy rhythm guitars, propulsive live drums, locked bass, one memorable lead guitar motif, wider group vocals in the chorus, raw but controlled lead vocal, analog warmth.

## Exclude Styles
Screamed verses, endless guitar solos, muddy distortion, electronic drops, and hateful messaging.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Verse]
We built a wall from little words
Left all the harder ones unheard
But I can feel the morning move
Like fire through a darkened room
[Chorus]
Break the silence, let it fall
Every whisper finds the wall
Break the silence, say it loud
We are stronger than the doubt
```

## Expected Result
A clear riff, strong downbeats, and a wide chorus that can be sung collectively.

## Failure Modes
Unintelligible verses, a weak drum groove, or guitar layers that hide the hook.

## Next Iteration
Reduce the verse to one guitar and bass, then request a chorus-only arrangement lift.

## Evidence
Evidence tier: `working-hypothesis`.
