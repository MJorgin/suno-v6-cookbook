# Mumbled Chinese or English Lyrics

## Symptom
Words blur, English syllables land awkwardly, or Chinese lines lose their natural stress and vowel shape.

## Likely cause
The line has too many syllables, both languages switch inside one phrase, or dense lyrics sit on fast passing notes.

## Conservative repair
Shorten each line, give each phrase one breath, and keep one language dominant per section. Test diction with `v6-mini`.

## Exploratory repair
Turn the secondary language into a background answer or wordless harmony, then rebuild the lead with `v6`.
