# Cantopop Electropop

## Identity

A sleek city-pop-influenced blend of Cantonese melody, modern synth bass, polished drums, and compact emotional hooks.

## Default model

`v6` for Cantonese phrasing and controlled production; `v6-wild` for synth texture experiments.

## Useful descriptors

Neon Hong Kong night, glassy synthesizers, tight electronic drums, melodic bass, bittersweet chorus, clean city sheen, midtempo groove, airy harmonies.

## Instrumentation

Synth bass, crisp snare or clap, arpeggiated synthesizer, rhythm guitar stabs, spacious pads, and occasional piano for emotional contrast.

## Vocal direction

Bright but emotionally controlled. Use light syncopation in verses and a longer, open chorus melody. Avoid forcing Mandarin pronunciation patterns into Cantonese lyrics.

## Rhythm and production

Around 92–112 BPM. Keep the low end tight, pan synth textures wide, and leave the center clear for the lead vocal.

## Avoid

Overly long Cantonese lines, retro synth presets without a modern beat, harsh autotune effects, dense English rap, and more than two competing synth riffs.

## Chinese/English notes

Write for sound first. Check that final particles and rhyme words land in singable positions. English words should be short and rhythmically locked to the synth groove.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original Cantonese electropop, bittersweet neon-city mood. Glassy synths, tight electronic drums, melodic synth bass, rhythm guitar stabs, airy harmonies. Midtempo groove with a clean modern mix and a wide open chorus. Bright controlled lead vocal with natural Cantonese phrasing. Avoid retro disco clichés, harsh vocal effects, and crowded verses.

[Chorus]
< concise Cantonese hook with a clear ending rhyme >
```
