# Launch Note: Awesome Suno v6 Cookbook

**Awesome Suno v6 Cookbook** is an open, bilingual, evidence-labeled cookbook for creating music with Suno v6.

The project helps creators move beyond trial-and-error prompting by organizing v6 into practical routes: model selection, first-song prompts, local lyric edits, section replacement, voice memos, visual inputs, sampling, isolation, owned-source mashups, Chinese singability, and failure repair.

## What is inside

- 12 end-to-end workflow recipes
- 12 genre prompt playbooks
- 24 original copy-ready examples
- 6 focused failure notes
- 1 dependency-free Markdown agent skill
- Bilingual English and Chinese entry points

## Why it matters

Suno v6 introduces more ways to create and edit, but more model choices and input types can also make prompts harder to debug. The cookbook makes those capabilities easier to approach by separating official facts from practical hypotheses and by showing creators what to preserve at each step.

## Safety stance

The project is independent and community maintained. It does not provide upload automation or account access. It encourages creators to use only material they own or have permission to use, avoid unauthorized samples, and avoid requests that impersonate real people.
