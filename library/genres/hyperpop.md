# Hyperpop

## Identity

Playful, high-density electronic pop built from bright synths, exaggerated vocals, rapid arrangement changes, and internet-era energy.

## Default model

`v6-wild` for experimentation, then `v6` to make the chaos coherent.

## Useful descriptors

Glossy metallic synths, pitch-shifted accents, bubblegum melody, crunchy drums, fast edits, cute but aggressive, maximalist hooks, elastic bass.

## Instrumentation

Saw or bell synth leads, elastic 808/kick, snare rushes, colorful sound effects, chopped original vocal fragments, and two-bar texture changes.

## Vocal direction

Bright, manipulated, playful, and extreme only where intentional. Keep one lead vocal understandable; reserve chipmunk or robot layers for answers and ad-libs.

## Rhythm and production

Around 140–170 BPM or a half-time feel that sounds equivalent. Use sharp edits, dynamic contrast, and a simple hook hidden inside maximal production.

## Avoid

Every bar changing key, unreadable main lyrics, harsh distortion for the whole track, more than three vocal effects, and mean-spirited shock content.

## Chinese/English notes

Short Chinese phrases with hard consonants can punch through the mix. English ad-libs add energy, but the main hook should stay in one language.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original hyperpop song, cute but aggressive, bright metallic synths, elastic bass, crunchy drums, fast two-bar edits, playful pitch-shifted ad-libs, one understandable lead vocal and a simple bubblegum hook. Keep key stable and avoid harsh distortion on the whole track.
```

## Evidence

Evidence tier: `working-hypothesis`; the genre's exaggerated effects need careful iteration and taste checks.
