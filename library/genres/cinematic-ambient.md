# Cinematic Ambient

## Identity

Slow, spacious music for image, memory, tension, or atmosphere where texture and progression matter more than a conventional hook.

## Default model

`v6-wild` for texture and `v6` for controlled scene timing.

## Useful descriptors

Deep space, soft piano, evolving pads, distant textures, gradual lift, restrained tension, wide reverb, low pulse, emotional restraint.

## Instrumentation

Long pads, soft piano fragments, cello or low strings, sub-bass pulse, granular texture, brushed percussion, and sparse bell or chime tones.

## Vocal direction

Use wordless vocals, humming, distant choir-like layers, or no vocals. If lyrics are present, keep them minimal and secondary to texture.

## Rhythm and production

Tempo can be free or slow. Build through timbre and register rather than a drum loop. Define entry points, peak moment, and ending silence.

## Avoid

A drum beat arriving without reason, constant cymbal swells, five melodic instruments playing themes simultaneously, and trailer-hit overuse.

## Chinese/English notes

For Chinese vocals, prefer single words or short repeated lines rather than dense poetry. English wordless syllables can blend into pads more forgivingly.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original cinematic ambient cue for <scene/emotion>. Evolving warm pads, sparse piano fragments, low cello or sub-bass pulse, distant granular texture, wide reverb, gradual lift after one minute, restrained ending. Wordless or minimal vocals; no sudden rock beat or trailer hit.
```
