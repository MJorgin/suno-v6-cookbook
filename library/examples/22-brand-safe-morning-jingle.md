## Goal
Create a cheerful, original 15-second jingle suitable for a generic breakfast brand without mentioning a company name.

## Model
`v6` for a clean, repeatable melody.

## Inputs
Duration, mood, family-safe lyrics, and acoustic indie palette.

## Style Prompt
Original upbeat indie-pop jingle, acoustic guitar, light shaker, soft claps, warm bass, whistle-like synth optional, sunny and friendly, simple melody, clean children-and-family-safe mood.

## Exclude Styles
Named brands, ironic humor, heavy beats, sexual content, aggressive guitars, and complex lyrics.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
Create a 15-second original jingle.
[Hook]
Morning light, open wide
Something good is by your side
Little bite, little glow
Start the day and let it grow

End with two instrumental bars that can loop under voice-over.
```

## Expected Result
A memorable, neutral, brand-safe melody with a simple rhyme and usable instrumental tail.

## Failure Modes
Lyrics imply a specific brand, the hook is too wordy, or the tone becomes childish or sarcastic.

## Next Iteration
Reduce lyrics to two lines, make the final melody solfège-neutral, and request a 15-second and 30-second version.

Evidence tier: `official-informed`.
