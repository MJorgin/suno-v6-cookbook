# Contributor Covenant-Style Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We keep this repository focused on safe, original, and well-sourced AI music
prompting. We welcome contributors of all musical backgrounds, languages, and
skill levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Give feedback on prompts, evidence, and workflow quality, not on people.
- Respect copyright, trademark, privacy, and platform terms.
- Label official facts, practical inference, community tests, and guesses
  separately.
- Do not submit private media, leaked audio, commercial lyrics, or instructions
  for impersonating a real person without permission.
- Help improve prompts for users working in languages other than English.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, doxxing, or deliberately intimidating contributors.
- Sharing unauthorized copyrighted recordings, stems, samples, or lyrics.
- Providing stolen credentials, paid account sharing, or API-resale links.
- Claiming community-tested or speculative prompts are official guarantees.
- Using the issue tracker for commercial spam or unsupported service ads.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, remove, or reject contributions that violate these
rules. Reports may be opened privately through a repository issue if no private
contact address is configured. Clear copyright or impersonation reports receive
priority and content may be removed while the claim is reviewed.
