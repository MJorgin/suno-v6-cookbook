## Goal
Create a relaxed English bossa/jazz-pop song about sharing a secret on an afternoon train.

## Model
`v6` for harmony, soft swing, and vocal/instrument balance.

## Inputs
Original English lyrics, café intimacy, and small-combo instrumentation.

## Style Prompt
Original relaxed bossa jazz-pop, nylon-string guitar, warm jazz piano, round upright bass, brushed drums and soft ride, occasional muted trumpet after the second chorus, intimate lead vocal slightly behind the beat, rich seventh chords.

## Exclude Styles
Loud electronic drums, nonstop horn solo, aggressive swing, rock guitar, and crowded reverb.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Verse]
Train window frames a silver sky
You tell me where the old days hide
Coffee steam becomes a cloud
We laugh a little, not too loud
[Chorus]
Keep the secret soft and slow
Where the summer trains still go
```

## Expected Result
Light syncopation, clear words, and sophisticated harmony without vocal strain.

## Failure Modes
Horns cover the vocal, drums become too loud, or harmony changes distract from the melody.

## Next Iteration
Remove the trumpet until the outro and ask for guitar and bass only in the first verse.

Evidence tier: `working-hypothesis`.
