# Weak Chorus Hook

## Symptom
The verse is pleasant, but the chorus has no phrase to remember and sounds like more verse.

## Likely cause
The chorus lacks repetition, a stable vowel sound, a clear title phrase, or an arrangement lift.

## Conservative repair
Write a six-to-ten-syllable title phrase, repeat it twice, reduce verse instrumentation, and request a chorus-only section replacement with `v6`.

## Exploratory repair
Generate three alternate choruses with `v6-wild`; keep only the strongest melodic shape, then rewrite lyrics to fit it with `v6`.
