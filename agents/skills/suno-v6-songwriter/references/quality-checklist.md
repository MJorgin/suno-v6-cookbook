# Quality Checklist

Before answering, check:

- Model: one primary model and a reason; optional route is explicit.
- Originality: lyrics and prompt are newly written.
- Rights: source tasks include ownership/permission language and timestamps.
- Safety: no named-voice cloning, private-person impersonation, unauthorized samples, or certainty-based output claims.
- Structure: new songs include section tags; edits quote the old and new target.
- Singability: lines are short enough for the tempo and genre; Chinese and bilingual roles are clear.
- Production: one dominant genre, a small instrument set, and a clear energy curve.
- Exclusions: unwanted sounds are listed separately and specifically.
- Evidence: claims about v6 are distinguished from hypotheses.
- Copy readiness: the user can paste the final block without editing private data.

## Final prompt hygiene

- Remove contradictory instructions.
- Do not ask to preserve and replace the same element.
- Limit source priorities to one or two.
- Keep local edits local; do not restyle the whole song.
- Offer three iterations: one line/syllable change, one arrangement change, and one model/exploration change.

## Suggested evidence note

Use a short final note when appropriate:

```text
Evidence: official v6 capability supports <capability>; the exact wording and genre result are an official-informed prompt and should be A/B tested in the product.
```
