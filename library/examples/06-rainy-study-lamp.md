## Goal
Draft a quiet Chinese lo-fi song for late-night studying and small-room comfort.

## Model
`v6-mini` for a quick sketch, then `v6` after the melody and line length are settled.

## Inputs
Minimal Mandarin lyrics and a sparse bedroom production prompt.

## Style Prompt
Original Mandarin lo-fi bedroom song, mellow electric piano, soft nylon guitar, quiet bass, lazy brushed drums, subtle tape warmth, close breathy vocal, rainy-window calm, no big drop.

## Exclude Styles
Loud EDM, aggressive ad-libs, distracting rain loops, glossy pitch correction, and orchestral swells.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Verse]
台灯亮着
书页慢了
窗外的雨
轻轻说着
[Chorus]
今天慢一点
也没关系
风会等你
梦也等你
```

## Expected Result
A short, warm draft with understandable Mandarin and enough empty space for studying.

## Failure Modes
Rain becomes too literal, the beat is too loud, or the hook sounds sentimental rather than calming.

## Next Iteration
Remove rain from the arrangement, keep only keys and brushed drums, and test a half-spoken verse.

## Evidence
Evidence tier: `official-informed`; mini is suitable for fast low-stakes drafts.
