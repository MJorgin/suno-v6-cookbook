# Rock Anthem

## Identity

Guitar-driven, large-stage song with a strong riff, forward momentum, and a chorus designed for collective singing.

## Default model

`v6`; use `v6-wild` only for a stranger riff or bridge.

## Useful descriptors

Crunch guitars, live drums, propulsive bass, arena-wide chorus, gang vocals, dynamic verse, defiant but hopeful energy, analog warmth.

## Instrumentation

Distorted rhythm guitars, lead guitar motif, live drum kit, driving bass, occasional acoustic layer in verses, and group vocals in the chorus.

## Vocal direction

Raw but controlled, with clear words and enough sustain for the chorus. Emotion should come from conviction, not constant shouting.

## Rhythm and production

Around 118–140 BPM. Use strong downbeats, clear drum breaks, a bass-guitar lock, and a chorus wider and louder than the verse.

## Avoid

Nonstop guitar solos, muddy distortion, screamed verses with no melody, drum machines that fight live drums, and political hate content.

## Chinese/English notes

Keep Chinese hook lines short and open; long phrases become difficult above distorted guitars. English shouts work as background answers, not the entire melody.

## Mini recipe

```text
Original uplifting rock anthem about <resistance or renewal>. Crunch rhythm guitars, propulsive live drums, locked bass, one memorable lead guitar motif, wider group vocals in the chorus. Raw controlled lead vocal with a clear sustained hook. Avoid endless solos, muddy distortion, and unintelligible verses.
```
