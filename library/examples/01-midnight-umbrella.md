## Goal
Create a polished Mandarin ballad about two people separating quietly after a rainy commute.

## Model
`v6` for controlled diction, emotional continuity, and a polished chorus.

## Inputs
Original Chinese lyrics, text-only style direction, and a restrained arrangement plan.

## Style Prompt
Original Mandarin ballad, warm piano in the verse, soft pads, modern slow drums entering before the chorus, later strings, intimate and restrained lead vocal, wide but clean final chorus.

## Exclude Styles
Rap, EDM drops, theatrical opera vocals, heavy guitar, and crowded percussion.

## Lyrics or Edit Instruction
```text
[Verse]
雨停在路口
灯把夜照透
你收起雨伞
没有说以后
[Pre-Chorus]
风经过衣袖
把再见吹得很轻
[Chorus]
我在雨后想起你
像城市忽然安静
那盏没关的灯
还照着两个人的背影
```

## Expected Result
A clear verse-to-chorus lift, natural Mandarin pronunciation, and a repeatable emotional hook.

## Failure Modes
Over-long lines, exaggerated singing, or strings entering too early and burying the voice.

## Next Iteration
Shorten the chorus to eight-character phrases if diction is unclear, then ask for a local line edit rather than regenerating the whole song.
