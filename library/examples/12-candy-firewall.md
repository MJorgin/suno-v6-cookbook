## Goal
Develop a playful hyperpop concept about online shyness, then refine the chaotic idea into one coherent hook.

## Model
`v6-wild` for maximal edits, followed by `v6` to restore key stability.

## Inputs
A short bilingual hook, bright synth direction, and strict limits on vocal effects.

## Style Prompt
Original hyperpop, glossy metallic bells, elastic bass, crunchy drums, fast two-bar edits, cute but aggressive energy, playful manipulated answer vocals, one understandable lead, bubblegum hook, stable key.

## Exclude Styles
Harsh full-track distortion, random key changes, mean-spirited lyrics, more than three vocal effects, and unintelligible main hook.
